About the team

Viyan R&D brings together three electrical engineering students at Isfahan University of Technology. Our work covers firmware for microcontrollers, PCB design and simulation, long-range wireless networks, signal processing and blockchain development. The team also goes by Madarafzar Hooshmand Isfahan.
